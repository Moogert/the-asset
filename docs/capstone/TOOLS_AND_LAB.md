# Tools and lab access

[Trello equipment card](https://trello.com/c/CNCl5bQS)

This is an equipment requirement list, not a purchase approval. **Models, ratings,
ownership and calibration are unverified.** Use borrowing or qualified lab services
where practical. Select ratings from the actual hardware and measurement range.

## Equipment schedule

| Group | Equipment | Purpose / required capability |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical | Lift, stands, chocks and lifting fixture | Rated support for the vehicle and drivetrain; reviewed lifting points |
| Mechanical | Torque wrenches and sockets | Required torque range and current calibration; correct service specifications |
| Mechanical | Measuring and axle/brake tools | Dimensions, clearances, joint travel, bearing/axle service and brake bleeding/pressure checks |
| Harness | Crimpers, dies and extraction tools | Match actual terminals; inspected sample crimps and retention |
| Harness | Breakouts and connector supplies | Removable interfaces, strain relief and clear cavity/wire labels |
| Harness | Rework tools and bench supply | Suitable soldering/magnification and fused, current-limited low-voltage power |
| Electrical | Meter, leads and voltage verification | Actual voltage/category ratings; calibrated measurement and verified voltage absence |
| Electrical | Insulation tester | Appropriate test voltage and procedure protecting connected electronics |
| Electrical | Protective equipment and insulated tools | Ratings, condition and use defined by the lab's electrical procedure |
| Controls | Oscilloscope and differential/isolated probes | Required common-mode voltage, bandwidth and safe grounding arrangement |
| Controls | Current probes and logic analyzer | Suitable range, resolution, bandwidth, polarity and calibration |
| Controls | CAN and programming interfaces | Correct transceivers/voltage levels and support for selected controllers |
| Performance | Torque and independent speed sensors | Calibrated measurement of both output speeds and shaft/reaction torque |
| Performance | Synchronized acquisition | Declared timing uncertainty, sample bandwidth, channel alignment and raw-data retention |
| Performance | Temperature and flow instruments | Installed oil/motor/inverter/coolant measurements with verified sensor curves |
| Performance | Rated load equipment and cooling | Torque-speed-power envelope, low-speed absorption, heat rejection and guarding |

Confirm access and suitability before scheduling commissioning. A general-purpose
Arduino or serial logger does not replace isolated measurement, calibrated fast
capture or independent protection. An ordinary bench supply may not absorb
regenerated energy. These requirements do not authorize energized testing.
